# Product family

Three apps live under this folder:

| Folder | Product | Domain |
|--------|---------|--------|
| [ShiftSwift](./ShiftSwift/) | Snapshot of the live **ShiftSwift HR** web app | Workforce HR, clock, sponsor licence |
| [SwiftCRM](./SwiftCRM/) | **Lettings CRM** | Today, lettings, inbox, money — a small agency desk |
| [SwiftHelp](./SwiftHelp/) | **IT helpdesk** | Today, tickets, assets, inbox — a small office IT desk |

The production ShiftSwift HR codebase remains at the **repository root**. The copy here is a frozen app snapshot so products can be compared side by side.

Open the family launcher: `products/index.html`.
