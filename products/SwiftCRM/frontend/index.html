What the desk actually needs

Xero and FreeAgent are the accounts pack. SwiftCRM is the lettings operating system that feeds them.

Portfolio

Flats, houses, HMOs and studios — rent, landlord, EPC notes, let or vacant.

Tenants & landlords

Separate records. The agency communicates with both — email, SMS, phone, or the tenant/landlord apps.

Tenancies

AST dates, rent, deposit. The record that invoices hang off.

Invoices & pay

Issue rent, mark overdue, collect Bacs or card, queue a Xero sync.

Lettings pipeline

Enquiry → viewing → offer → referencing → move-in. Sales CRM shape, housing stages.

Local tax

Council tax band, billing authority and who is liable. Landlord UTR and HMRC non-resident landlord status on file.

Separate on purpose

Same company. Two products. Do not mix staff clock-ins with landlord client money.
