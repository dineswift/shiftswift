# SwiftHelp — product concept

**Legal entity:** Datasoftware Analytics Ltd (Co. 14568900)  
**Trading name:** SwiftHelp  
**Sibling products:** SwiftCRM (lettings) · ShiftSwift HR (workforce) · DineSwift (hospitality)

SwiftHelp is **not** a mode inside SwiftCRM and **not** a re-skin of ShiftSwift HR. It is a separate IT helpdesk for a small internal team or a one-person IT shop.

---

## Who it is for

Office IT that currently runs on email + a spreadsheet of laptops.

They need: who raised it, which device, what is due, who to tell.

## Who it is not for

- Lettings / landlord money (that is SwiftCRM)
- Employees, punch, rota, RTW (that is ShiftSwift HR)
- ServiceNow, full ITIL, CMDB, change advisory boards
- Live email / Slack / Jira sync in this slice

---

## Five jobs

1. **Today** — P1s, SLA due, waiting on the user
2. **Tickets** — the record (requester + asset + thread)
3. **Assets** — laptops, phones, printers
4. **Inbox** — updates to the requester
5. **Settings**

---

## Colour

Charcoal chrome, amber CTAs, cool paper. Not HR green, not lettings copper.
