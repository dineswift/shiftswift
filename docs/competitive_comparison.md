## Positioning in one sentence

**ShiftSwift HR** is UK workforce HR for restaurants, care, retail, and **sponsor licence holders** — employee records, geofenced clock-in, rotas, and Home Office audit trails in one product, without running payroll or HMRC RTI.

We are **not** trying to beat Personio or BambooHR on breadth. We **are** trying to beat the real incumbent: **spreadsheets + WhatsApp + a separate clock app + panic before a Home Office audit**.

---

## Who each option is for

| Option | Best for | Weak fit when |
|--------|----------|---------------|
| **Spreadsheets + folders** | Very small teams, no sponsor licence, owner does everything | Sponsor licence, disputes over hours, multi-manager access, RTW expiry risk |
| **Rotacloud** (scheduling) | Rota-first venues that only need shifts + basic clock | RTW evidence, sponsor day-9, audit export packs, employee lifecycle |
| **Breathe HR** (general SME HR) | Office-style SMEs wanting familiar HR records + policies | Sponsor-specific duties, clock-linked day-9, geofence + QR punch audit trail |
| **BrightHR + BrightPay** | Businesses already on BrightPay wanting one brand | Same as Breathe for sponsor depth; payroll is separate product anyway |
| **Deputy / Workforce.com** | Large shift-based ops, strong mobile scheduling | UK sponsor compliance as first-class; cost at smaller headcount |
| **ShiftSwift HR** | UK venues with **compliance risk** (RTW, sponsor, disputes) who want **clock + HR + audit** together | Pure rota-only cafés with no compliance exposure; enterprises needing SOC 2 pack day one |

## When to recommend ShiftSwift HR

Lead with ShiftSwift when the buyer says any of:

- “We hold a **sponsor licence**” or “Home Office could visit”
- “Clock-in disputes eat our time” or “staff clock in from the car park”
- “RTW checks live in WhatsApp and a folder”
- “We use a rota app **and** spreadsheets for HR”
- “Accountant asks for hours and we rebuild them every month”

Do **not** force ShiftSwift when:

- Single-site café, no sponsorship, only needs a cheap rota → Rotacloud may suffice
- Office SME, no clock, wants polished generic HR → Breathe is a fair choice
- Enterprise procurement requiring SOC 2 Day 1 → partner or roadmap conversation

## Objection handling (short)

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| “Breathe already does HR” | “For generic records, yes. We link **geofenced clock-in to day-9 alerts** and ship a **Home Office audit export** — that’s the gap sponsors hit.” |
| “We already use Rotacloud” | “Keep it for pure scheduling if you want — but RTW expiry, sponsor reporting, and punch dispute trails still live elsewhere. ShiftSwift replaces that second stack.” |
| “Spreadsheets work fine” | “Until an audit or pay dispute. Our punch log shows distance, QR vs GPS, and flags rapid re-punches for HR — spreadsheets can’t.” |
| “Do you run payroll?” | “No — by design. We export hours your accountant or BrightPay uses. That keeps HR subscription simple and avoids RTI scope creep.” |
| “Are you mature enough?” | “Early access honest: live on UK infrastructure, 14-day trial, pilot customers. Judge the compliance workflow in a demo, not a Gartner quadrant.” |
