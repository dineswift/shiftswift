      How leave works

      • Employee portal — staff submit annual, sick, unpaid, or other leave from their dashboard.
      • Working days — Mon–Fri are counted automatically; weekends are excluded.
      • Annual balance — approving annual leave checks the employee’s remaining allowance for the year.
      • Sponsored workers — approved leave is mirrored to sponsor absence tracking where applicable.
