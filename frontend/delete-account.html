Delete your account and data

Last updated: 7 September 2026 · ShiftSwift HR app (co.uk.shiftswifthr.app)

Use this page to request account deletion and/or data deletion for ShiftSwift HR. Google Play Data safety links for this app point here.

1. How to request deletion

You can request deletion in either of these ways:

  1. Email support@shiftswifthr.co.uk with the subject Account and data deletion request, or
  2. Complete the form below. It opens an email with the details filled in.

Privacy queries: legal@datasoftwareanalytics.co.uk.

We verify that the request comes from the account holder (or an authorised business contact), then complete deletion within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation email.

Deletion request form

If the button does not open email, write to support@shiftswifthr.co.uk.

2. What we delete

After we verify the request, we delete or disable:

  • Your ShiftSwift HR app login and portal account
  • Push notification subscriptions and device tokens for that account
  • Sign-in logs and profile details we hold as controller (for example a trial signup in your own name)
  • Local app data on this device after you uninstall the app

3. What we may keep

  • Billing and tax records — typically up to 7 years where UK law requires it
  • Security logs — typically up to 90 days
  • Employee HR records (identity, RTW, rotas, punches) — your employer is the data controller. We delete or anonymise that data when the employer instructs us, or when the business workspace itself is closed

4. Employee app users

If you signed in because your employer invited you, deleting your app account removes your login. Employment records stay with your employer until they delete or export them. Ask your HR manager as well as sending this request so both the app account and the HR file are handled.

In the Employee app you can also open More → Delete account and data, which loads this page.

5. Business / HR admin accounts

A verified owner request can close the workspace: admin logins, employee portal access, and tenant data we store for that business, except records we must keep for tax or legal reasons.
