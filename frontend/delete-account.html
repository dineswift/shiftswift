Delete your ShiftSwift HR account

ShiftSwift HR · Datasoftware Analytics Ltd · Last updated: 11 July 2026

Use this page to request deletion of a ShiftSwift HR account, or to request deletion of some or all personal data while keeping the account open. You do not need to keep the mobile app installed to make either request.

  • Delete some or all data (keep account)
  • Delete the whole account

Request data deletion (keep your account)

You can ask us to delete some or all personal data we hold about you as controller without closing your ShiftSwift HR account.

  1. Email legal@datasoftwareanalytics.co.uk from the email address registered on the account.
  2. Use the subject line ShiftSwift HR data deletion request and include:
    • Full name and account email / username
    • Business / organisation name (if applicable)
    • Which data you want deleted (e.g. profile fields, notification tokens, support tickets)
    • Confirm that you want to keep the account open

Email data deletion request

How to request full account deletion

  1. Email legal@datasoftwareanalytics.co.uk from the email address registered on the account.
  2. Use the subject line ShiftSwift HR account deletion request and include:
    • Full name
    • Account email / username
    • Business / organisation name (if applicable)
    • Whether you use the Android app, iOS app, and/or web portal
  3. You can also write to support@shiftswifthr.co.uk; we will route the request to our privacy team.

Email account deletion request

What we delete

After we verify the request, we delete or anonymise personal data we control as requested, including where applicable:

  • Login credentials and profile details (for full account deletion)
  • Selected profile or contact fields (for data-only requests)
  • App device tokens used for notifications
  • Support correspondence linked to that account (where not required for legal retention)

What may be retained

  • Billing / invoice records — kept for statutory retention (typically up to 7 years in the UK).
  • Employee HR records processed for an employer — your employer is usually the data controller. Ask them (or their HR admin) to delete or amend employment data held in ShiftSwift HR. We act as processor under our Data Processing Addendum.
  • Security / audit logs — may be retained for a limited period (typically up to 90 days) where needed for security or fraud prevention.
  • For a data-only request, account credentials and data you did not ask to remove stay so the account continues to work.

Timing

We aim to confirm receipt within a few working days and complete verified deletion requests within 30 days, unless a longer period is required by law or to resolve an active dispute (for example unpaid invoices).

In the app

From the ShiftSwift HR app or web portal, you can also open this page or email legal@datasoftwareanalytics.co.uk to request deletion. See our Privacy policy for full details of how we handle personal data.
